CRM Project

GameZone CRM

Manages the gaming side of the business end-to-end — station bookings, live sessions, player billing, and real-time revenue tracking. The dashboard shows active stations, total players, and today's bookings at a glance, with a weekly revenue chart and a live view of which stations are occupied. Billing pulls session charges automatically, and staff can track payments by cash, UPI, or card. A full transaction history keeps every sale and booking accounted for.

CRM Project

PCW CRM RealEstate

A complete system for real estate businesses to manage their entire sales process in one place, even across multiple companies or branches. It keeps track of every property - plots and flats - showing clearly which ones are available, on hold, or already booked, so there's never any mix-up or accidental double booking. It also manages the full buyer journey: capturing new leads, assigning them to the right sales agent, scheduling site visits, and keeping a record of every follow-up and conversation. Buyer documents and ID verification are stored securely in one place, with reminders when any paperwork is about to expire. Installment schedules for buyers are recorded and tracked automatically, along with commission amounts owed to brokers and agents - cutting down on manual paperwork and reducing confusion between the sales team and partners. Agents and brokers also get their own private login to check their assigned leads and property availability in real time.

CRM Project

Cafe CRM

A dedicated system to manage a cafe's full operations. Menu organized by category, each item showing live stock, yesterday/today breakdown, sold-today count, with restock and low-stock alerts built in. Orders get placed and tracked through dedicated add/manage tools, with a running cart, customer field, and payment method before checkout. Earnings dashboard breaks revenue down by day, week, month, year, or custom range, shows top-selling items, flags low stock, and prints reports. Orders stay organized across in-process, completed, and full history, searchable by name, phone, or transaction ID.

Mobile App

NeighbourOS

NeighbourOS is an AI-powered society management platform built by PinCodedWeb Technology for gated communities and apartment societies. It replaces fragmented WhatsApp groups, manual logbooks, and phone calls with unified, role-based dashboards for everyone in the society.

Residents pre-approve visitors with digital gate passes, pay dues online, book amenities and EV charging slots, raise complaints, trigger emergency SOS alerts, and ask an AI assistant questions based on society bylaws. Security guards check in visitors and deliveries, validate QR passes, and receive instant SOS alerts. Staff and vendors manage attendance, work orders, and recurring maintenance through a structured workflow. The management committee gets one dashboard for billing, accounting, payroll, vendor contracts, and analytics.
